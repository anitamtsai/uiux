Cognitive Offload

An AI-powered external executive function system that eliminates cognitive burden by capturing messy thoughts with zero friction and organizing them automatically. Rather than adding another productivity tool, Cognitive Offload reimagines how knowledge workers manage mental energy and attention throughout their day.

The Problem: Cognitive Overload Crisis

Knowledge workers today face an impossible paradox: they use an average of 9.4 different tools daily, yet 72% report feeling cognitively overwhelmed. The problem isn't a lack of tools—it's that every thought creates a decision point.

The Choice Every Thought Forces

Why Current Solutions Fail

Capture Friction

Task managers require upfront categorization at capture time. Users must decide folder, priority, and deadline before the system helps—consuming cognitive energy on organization instead of work.

Manual Organization

Users do the sorting work. What should be automated is delegated to the human. This ongoing maintenance burden keeps growing with every new task.

Ignores Mental State

All apps show identical interface all day. Yet cognitive capacity varies 40-60% throughout the day. Morning energy ≠ post-meeting energy ≠ evening reflection state.

Context Loss

Tasks captured weeks ago lose meaning. "Why does this matter?" becomes a 5-10 minute manual reconstruction effort instead of instant clarity.

"I have so many apps to organize my life, but organizing them has become my job. I just want somewhere to dump thoughts and have them handled."
— Sarah, Project Manager (22 interviews conducted)

Design Philosophy: Never Create More Load

The AI should never create more cognitive load than it removes. Every feature, every suggestion, every interaction must reduce mental burden.

This principle cascaded into six core design rules that guided every decision:

Six Core Design Principles

The Solution: Three Core Views

Three interconnected interfaces solve the cognitive load problem at different moments of the workflow. Each view serves a distinct need while working together as a cohesive system.

Focus View: Daily Dashboard

The primary interface where users spend 95% of their time. Tasks organized not by deadline, but by cognitive effort required.

  • Deep Work (1-2 hrs): High-focus tasks shown prominently in morning when energy peaks.
  • Admin (30 min): Lower-friction work perfect for post-meeting recovery.
  • Anytime (5-10 min): Discrete, low-focus tasks for quick breaks.
  • Always-Present Mic: Capture button accessible at bottom without switching views.

Mental Inbox: Zero-Friction Capture

A simple, distraction-free repository for raw thoughts. Tap mic, speak your thought, immediately return to work.

  • Voice-Primary Input: Record thoughts in 2 seconds on the go.
  • Text & Paste Support: Copy-paste emails, Slack messages, or links.
  • Zero Decisions: No folders, dates, or priorities needed upfront.

All Tasks: Complete Inventory

For when users want complete visibility. Fully organized and searchable, showing the complete inventory of extracted tasks.

  • Organized by Timeframe: Clear views for this week, upcoming, and archive.
  • Full Context Display: Shows related messages, events, and documents.
  • Powerful Search: Find anything instantly across all ideas and context.

Core Capabilities

🎤 Voice Capture

Tap mic, record in 2 seconds, return to work immediately. Works anywhere with zero friction.

🧠 Auto Task Extraction

"That auth bug from standup" → "Fix auth logout bug." >90% accuracy extraction from messy speech.

⚡ Cognitive Effort Tagging

AI estimates effort for each task, grouping items by mental capacity rather than arbitrary priority.

🎯 Opportunity Surfacing

Surfaces relevant tasks when conditions align (free time, location, and calendar context).

🔗 Context Recovery

Reopen a task: related emails, calendar events, decisions, and documents surface automatically.

📊 Stale Detection

Automatically flags untouched items after 2 weeks to prevent mental debt build-up.
